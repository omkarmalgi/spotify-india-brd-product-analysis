## Project Overview

This project explores opportunities to improve user engagement and listening time on Spotify India through market research, competitor analysis, product insights and structured business requirements.

The central business challenge is to understand why users experience shorter listening sessions and identify requirements that could improve content discovery, engagement, retention and business outcomes.

## Business Problem

The project examines several engagement challenges:

- Repetitive music recommendations.
- Early drop-offs during listening sessions.
- Difficulty discovering regional and relevant music.
- Friction caused by interruptions in the free-tier experience.

## Project Objectives

- Understand the Indian music streaming market.
- Review competitor offerings and user experience.
- Identify customer pain points and product opportunities.
- Map current and proposed user processes.
- Define business, functional and non-functional requirements.
- Establish KPIs and project success criteria.

## Product Opportunity Areas

The research identifies four areas for further product exploration:

1. **Playlist Discovery:** Improve discovery of regional and relevant music.
2. **Listening Sessions:** Reduce friction that causes premature session endings.
3. **Offline Mode:** Address user frustrations around offline listening.
4. **Regional Content Engagement:** Improve engagement with Hindi, Punjabi, Tamil and Telugu content.

## Proposed Success Criteria

The BRD defines the following project targets:

| KPI | Proposed Target |
|---|---:|
| Average listening time per active user | Increase by 15% |
| User retention | Improve by 10% |
| Premium subscription conversion | Increase by 5% |
| Early session drop-offs | Reduce by 20% |

These are proposed targets, not measured or achieved results.
